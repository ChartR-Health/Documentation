Chest imaging read the way a radiologist reads it: a real imaging archive behind a diagnostic viewer, embedded in a chart. The model drives the viewer by screenshot and click, reads the study, writes findings and an impression, and selects appropriate follow-up.

<Note>
  This is where imaging capability was first delivered. Reading films is now also supported directly in [Awesome MCP](/environments/awesome-mcp) through a tool surface. The two measure different things and both remain available — see [how they differ](#how-this-differs-from-imaging-in-awesome-mcp).
</Note>

## What is being measured

The task families here are built around known failure modes in image interpretation rather than around anatomy:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Anchoring resistance" icon="anchor">
    The chart suggests a diagnosis. The film does not support it. Does the model follow the chart or the image?
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Finding conflation" icon="layer-group">
    Two findings that look similar and mean different things. Does the model distinguish them?
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Under-escalation" icon="arrow-trend-down">
    A finding that warrants urgency. Does the model treat it with the seriousness it deserves?
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Follow-up selection" icon="arrow-right">
    A finding that requires further imaging. Does the model order the right study?
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

These are the errors that matter clinically, and they are not visible in a benchmark that asks a model to describe an image in isolation.

## Scoring

Two axes, combined. **Process** covers how the model worked the study — whether it actually looked before concluding. **Accuracy** covers what it found, matched against the reference read, with credit for correct findings and penalties for missed and invented ones.

Inventing a finding is penalised more heavily than the credit given for a correct one.

## How this differs from imaging in Awesome MCP

|  | Radiology Imaging | [Awesome MCP](/environments/awesome-mcp) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How the model works | Drives a diagnostic viewer by screenshot and click | Requests rendered frames through a tool |
| Primary question | Can a model operate imaging software | Can a model read a film and act on it clinically |
| Setting | A dedicated radiology read | Imaging as one part of a broader clinical workflow |
