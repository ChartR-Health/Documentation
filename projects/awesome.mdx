Awesome is our EHR replica: a working clinical store with real patients, real encounters, and the full lifecycle of notes, orders, and prescriptions.

What makes it a family rather than a single environment is that the **same clinical store is offered across four different surfaces**. The medicine stays constant; how a model reaches it changes. That isolates a question most benchmarks conflate — whether a model knows what to do, versus whether it can actually do it against a real interface.

## The four surfaces

## Why the pairing matters

Tasks in Awesome MCP and Awesome Interop are **twinned**: the same clinical work, with byte-identical grading material, enforced by a cross-environment equivalence test.

When a model scores well on the curated surface and poorly on the raw one, that gap is informative and attributable. It is not a difference in task difficulty or in grading — it is the cost of removing the tool list.

<Note>
  This is the clearest measurement we offer of the difference between clinical reasoning and clinical execution.
</Note>
