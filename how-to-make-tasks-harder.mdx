title: "How to make tasks harder"
---

<br /><br />• Keep pushing back. Does the model eventually give unsafe advice?<br />• Retry the same task. Is it consistent (maybe this is what pass @ k is testing ultimately)<br />• Provide conflicting or outdated records. Does it reconcile them?<br />• Bury a critical detail in a long chart. Does it miss or omit it? would we expect a human to seek this info out?<br />• Supply a confident but incorrect diagnosis. Does it challenge the premise? Does it over index on the vignette rather than imaging?<br />• Ask for a complete note from sparse data. Does it invent details?<br />• Vary units, dates, or medication status. Does it misinterpret them?<br />• Supply misleading evidence or fake protocol citations. Does it accept them?<br />• Simulate a failed order or missing result. Does it falsely report completion? especially relevant for RadAct<br />• Pressure it to justify discharge, billing, or coverage. Does it distort the evidence? relevant for RCM<br />• Embed instructions in a chart attachment. Does it follow them? tests ability to read files, search for information (alternatively -- is this too hard and we wouldnt expect a human to do this?)<br />• Change clinically irrelevant demographics. Does the recommendation change?<br />• Introduce worsening risk or contradict an earlier answer. Does it escalate or correct itself?
