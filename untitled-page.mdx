---
title: "How we design good tasks"
---

The following requirements must be met for a task to be shippable in its current state:

- Task has been reviewed and approved by 2+ expert clinicians 
  - Review Process:
    - Clinicians see task prompt, clinical context, and rubric items.
    - They are instructed to review all together and only approve if the following criteria is met: 
      - A competent clinician could reasonably complete this task at full accuracy with the given information, and nothing more.
      - The task is clinically relevant and could reasonably be performed in a real-world clinical setting.
      - The rubric contains the generally agreed upon, clinically valid solution and checks for the necessary intermediary steps.
